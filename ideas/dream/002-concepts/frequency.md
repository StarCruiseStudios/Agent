# Concept: Frequency

**Layer:** 002, Concepts
**Status:** Proposed

Frequency is how often a [claim](claim.md) appears, counted in occurrences, distinct sessions, and distinct projects. A test that fails three times in one session has 3 occurrences in 1 session.

Frequency is kept apart from [support](support.md) (principle 5). An assumption that five sessions repeat from one hand-written instruction appears in five sessions and has one source. Layer 001 shows this case in its scenario about an assumption repeated from one source.
