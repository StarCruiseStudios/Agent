# Concept: Evidence

**Layer:** 002, Concepts
**Status:** Proposed

Evidence is what is cited for or against a [claim](claim.md), a [finding](finding.md), or a [change](change.md). [Observations](observation.md) and [user input](user-input.md) are evidence. An [inference](inference.md) can be cited, but it contributes only the evidence it rests on.

Evidence carries weight according to what it is. The observed record carries more weight than an agent's account of it (principle 1).

## Sameness

Two pieces of evidence are the same when they are the same observation or the same piece of user input.
