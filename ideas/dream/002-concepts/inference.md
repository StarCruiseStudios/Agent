# Concept: Inference

**Layer:** 002, Concepts
**Status:** Proposed

An inference is a conclusion that goes beyond what was observed, and every inference states a [claim](claim.md). Agents make inferences, which appear in the record as the content of what they say. Archon makes inferences too.

An agent's inference is a hypothesis until evidence supports it (principle 4). Inferences can build on other inferences, but a chain of them adds no [support](support.md) beyond the [observations](observation.md) and [user input](user-input.md) it finally rests on.

Deciding that two differently worded statements state the same claim is also an inference. In the layer 001 scenario about one cause across three agents, three sessions describe one problem in three ways, and Archon judges that they match. The judgment carries its own evidence and can be revised.

## Sameness

Two inferences are the same when they state the same claim from the same evidence.
