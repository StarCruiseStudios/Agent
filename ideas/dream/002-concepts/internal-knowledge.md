# Concept: Internal knowledge

**Layer:** 002, Concepts
**Status:** Proposed

Internal knowledge is everything Archon derives from the record and from user input. It includes [claims](claim.md), [inferences](inference.md), [sources](source.md), [support](support.md), [frequency](frequency.md), [findings](finding.md), and [questions](question.md). It also includes judgments about records, such as [tags](tag.md) that need judgment and who wrote a piece of [existing knowledge](existing-knowledge.md).

Archon changes internal knowledge freely (principle 12) and keeps its history (principle 10). Agents never load internal knowledge directly. They reach it only through [outputs](output.md).
