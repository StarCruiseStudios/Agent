# Concept: Measurement

**Layer:** 002, Concepts
**Status:** Proposed

A measurement is the measured effect of one or more [changes](change.md) on how agents work, such as the result of replaying comparable past tasks with and without a change (principle 16). It is linked to the changes it concerns and to the [records](record.md) it was made from, and a [proposal](proposal.md) can carry it.

A measurement is not [evidence](evidence.md). It shows what a change does, and it adds no [support](support.md) to any [claim](claim.md).

## Sameness

Each measurement is distinct. Two measurements of the same changes are two measurements.
