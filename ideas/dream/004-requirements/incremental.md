# Requirements: Incremental work

**Layer:** 004, Requirements
**Status:** Draft

These requirements let Archon take in new experience without reprocessing what it already holds, as principle 11 requires.

---

**INC-1. New records add to earlier work and never cause it to be repeated.** This follows from principle 11 and from the property in layer 003 that work is incremental.

- Collecting a new session rewrites no earlier record.
- A new record is associated against the whole set of records, and no association made earlier is rebuilt because the new record arrived (layer 003, Link).
- A new record changes an earlier claim, finding, or question only when the new record is evidence for or against it, or when the record it rests on is flagged as outdated.

**INC-2. Earlier work stays valid when nothing beneath it has changed.** This follows from principle 11.

- An association, claim, or finding whose records, associations, and evidence are unchanged keeps its current state when new records arrive.

**INC-3. Taking in new records repeats only the work they affect.** Principle 11 rules out reprocessing everything recorded before.

- Taking in a new record derives again only the claims, findings, and questions that the new record is evidence for or against, and those that rest on a record flagged as outdated because of it.
