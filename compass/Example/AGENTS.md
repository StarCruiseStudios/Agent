At the start of every new task or delegated subtask, consult Compass before 
planning or performing the work. Fetch an explicitly assigned pack directly; 
otherwise list packs and fetch the most relevant applicable pack. Always pass 
the absolute projectRoot. If no pack applies or the catalog is missing, continue
with the task. Do not fetch the pack directly unless you are executing the task.

If you cannot reach the Compass MCP, report that to the user and continue. Do 
not search for the pack files directly.
